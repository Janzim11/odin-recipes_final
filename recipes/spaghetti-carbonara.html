Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

Spaghetti carbonara is a classic Roman pasta dish. Eggs, hard cheese, crispy cured pork, and black pepper combine into a rich, creamy sauce without any cream.

The trick is to toss the hot pasta with the egg mixture off the heat so the sauce turns silky instead of scrambling.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 150 g guanciale or pancetta, diced
  • 3 large eggs plus 1 egg yolk
  • 1 cup freshly grated Pecorino Romano or Parmesan
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt, for the pasta water

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, fry the guanciale in a large pan over medium heat until crisp. Turn off the heat.
  3. Whisk the eggs, egg yolk, cheese, and plenty of black pepper in a bowl.
  4. Scoop out a cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti.
  5. Add the pasta to the pan with the guanciale and toss to coat in the fat.
  6. Add the egg mixture off the heat and toss quickly, adding splashes of pasta water until the sauce is creamy.
  7. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper.
