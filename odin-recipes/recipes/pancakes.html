Fluffy Pancakes

Description

These pancakes are thick, soft, and golden brown, perfect for a lazy weekend breakfast. The batter comes together in minutes using pantry staples.

Do not overmix the batter. A few lumps are fine and are the secret to light, fluffy pancakes.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter, plus more for the pan
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla.
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Leave a few lumps.
  4. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Heat a buttered pan or griddle over medium heat.
  6. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, about 2 minutes.
  7. Flip and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Serve warm with butter and maple syrup.
