Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken adobo is often called the unofficial national dish of the Philippines. Chicken is braised in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and peppercorns until it is tender and coated in a glossy, savory-tangy sauce.

It is simple to make, keeps well, and tastes even better the next day. Serve it over steamed white rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup white or cane vinegar
  • 1 head of garlic, crushed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns in a bowl and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat.
  3. Remove the chicken from the marinade (keep the marinade) and brown it on all sides, about 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in the marinade and the water, then bring to a boil.
  5. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 30 to 35 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  6. Uncover and simmer for another 10 minutes to thicken the sauce. Stir in the sugar if you like it slightly sweet.
  7. Serve hot over steamed rice.
